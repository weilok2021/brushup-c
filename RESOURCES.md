- [Bloch, Extra, Extra - Read All About It: Nearly All Binary Searches and Mergesorts are Broken (Google Research blog, 2 June 2006)](https://research.google/blog/extra-extra-read-all-about-it-nearly-all-binary-searches-and-mergesorts-are-broken/)

- [Wikipedia, Quicksort: Hoare and Lomuto partition schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quicksort#Hoare_partition_scheme)
  Language-free pseudocode. Verified 2026-09-17: Hoare scans `do i := i + 1 while A[i] < pivot`, `do j := j - 1 while A[j] > pivot`, `if i >= j then return j`; "When eventually the pointers cross (the first points after the second), no exchange is performed; a valid partition is found"; Hoare "does three times fewer swaps on average" than Lomuto and stays balanced on all-equal keys. Lomuto (`#Lomuto_partition_scheme`) picks the last element and "degrades to O(n²) runtime when all elements are equal". Use for: lesson 7 theory item and the one-scan versus two-scan comparison.
